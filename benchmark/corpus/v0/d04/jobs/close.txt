Frontend Engineer — Accessible product interfaces

Our technical team is seeking someone to improve accessible interaction states, component reuse, and responsive layout. The person in this role will own a bounded stream of work, record assumptions, and make tradeoffs visible before a release. Work includes investigating existing behavior, preparing a small change, and describing how another engineer can verify it. The team values plain explanations and careful attribution of contributions.

Required:

Hands-on experience with TypeScript and React; practical familiarity with CSS and Vitest.

The successful candidate can review interface behavior with design and support colleagues. They should be able to show a component change that can be understood and checked by other contributors. Examples may come from employment, internships, or substantial projects when those examples actually support the claim.

Preferred:

Experience documenting tradeoffs in accessible product interfaces and explaining changes to colleagues.

We appreciate concise evidence of what the applicant personally did, the tools used, and the result observed. We review applications for clear ownership, context, and an account of how the work was checked after release. Examples of shared work should explain the individual contribution.

This opening is designed for candidates whose work overlaps directly with the core stack. We value precise descriptions of work actually completed over broad claims about adjacent technologies.

The team works asynchronously, writes short implementation notes, and reviews changes with the people who will operate them. The application should make specific, verifiable experience easy to find without losing the context in which it happened.
