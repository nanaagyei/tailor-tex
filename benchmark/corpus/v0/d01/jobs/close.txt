Junior Backend Engineer — Backend service interfaces

Our technical team is seeking someone to improve service contracts, failure handling, and API maintenance. The person in this role will own a bounded stream of work, record assumptions, and make tradeoffs visible before a release. Work includes investigating existing behavior, preparing a small change, and describing how another engineer can verify it. The team values plain explanations and careful attribution of contributions.

Required:

Hands-on experience with Python and SQLite; practical familiarity with Git and pytest.

The successful candidate can review integration changes with engineers and support colleagues. They should be able to show a service change that is testable, documented, and simple to operate. Examples may come from employment, internships, or substantial projects when those examples actually support the claim.

Preferred:

Experience documenting tradeoffs in backend service interfaces and explaining changes to colleagues.

We appreciate concise evidence of what the applicant personally did, the tools used, and the result observed. We review applications for clear ownership, context, and an account of how the work was checked after release. Examples of shared work should explain the individual contribution.

This opening is designed for candidates whose work overlaps directly with the core stack. We value precise descriptions of work actually completed over broad claims about adjacent technologies.

The team works asynchronously, writes short implementation notes, and reviews changes with the people who will operate them. The application should make specific, verifiable experience easy to find without losing the context in which it happened.
